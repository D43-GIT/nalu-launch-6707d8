How it works

Four approvals, in order

  1. We send the concept. You approve it.
  2. We build reference frames of each location and render. You approve them for accuracy.
  3. We make the video.
  4. You get 1 round of changes per film. Extra rounds are quoted.

Timing

  • First hero film about 10 business days after the deposit, the high-res renders and floor plans, and the Traces of Being guide arrive.
  • Then a weekly drop: a hero film, short cuts and a carousel.
