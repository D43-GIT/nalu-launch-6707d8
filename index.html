How it works

Three approvals, in order.

  1. 1The concept

    We send the concept. You approve it.

  2. 2Reference frames

    We build reference frames of each location and render. You approve them for accuracy.

  3. 3Video and changes

    We make the video. You get 1 round of changes per film. Extra rounds are quoted.
